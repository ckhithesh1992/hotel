Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Hanuman Heritage ("we") handles your personal data when you use hanumanheritage.com, book a room or stay with us.

What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, mobile number, email (optional), dates of stay, number of guests and rooms booked
  • Payment details: the amount, booking ID and payment reference. Card, UPI PIN and bank details are handled by the payment provider and are never seen or stored by us.
  • At check-in: copies or details of government ID, as required by law for hotels in India
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, phone or email

Why we use it

  • To hold, confirm and manage your booking
  • To take payment and issue invoices, including GST records
  • To contact you about your stay
  • To meet legal duties, such as guest registers and reporting foreign guests to the authorities

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Our payment provider, to process payments
  • Google, which stores our booking records and email
  • Government authorities, where the law requires it

How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records for as long as tax and hotel laws require, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, unless we must keep it by law. Write to us at the contact below.

Cookies

The website saves your selected rooms and dates in your own browser so they are still there if you return. It does not use advertising cookies.
