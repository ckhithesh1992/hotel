Booking delivery policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

We provide hotel accommodation and event services. We do not ship any physical goods.

How you receive your booking

  • After you reserve online, you see your booking ID straight away and can print a booking slip.
  • When we receive your payment, we send your booking confirmation on WhatsApp, and by email if you gave one, within 24 hours and usually much sooner.
  • The service is delivered at the hotel on your check-in date, at Jyothinagar Main Road, Chikkamagaluru.

If you have paid but have not received a confirmation within 24 hours, contact us on +91 87925 64056 with your booking ID.
