A booking made on the website is held as "Pending Payment" until we receive full payment. It is confirmed when we receive the payment and send you a confirmation on WhatsApp or email. We may cancel an unpaid booking.

Room prices are per room per night in Indian rupees, as shown on our prices page. 5% GST is added. The total shown before payment is the amount you pay.
