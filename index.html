Experience Chikmagaluru

Chikmagaluru, nestled in the Western Ghats, is a paradise for nature lovers and coffee enthusiasts. Our hotel offers the perfect base to explore this beautiful hill station.

Nearby Attractions:

  • Mullayanagiri Peak - Highest peak in Karnataka
  • Coffee Plantations - Experience coffee culture
  • Hebbe Falls - Beautiful waterfall
  • Baba Budangiri - Scenic mountain range
  • Kemmangundi - Hill station retreat
