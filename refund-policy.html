Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Bookings paid online are non-refundable.

Cancellations

Once a booking is paid, it cannot be cancelled for a refund. This also applies to no-shows, late arrivals and early departures.

Changing your dates

You can change the dates of a paid booking once, free of charge, if rooms are available on the new dates. Ask us by WhatsApp or phone with your booking ID at least 24 hours before your check-in date. If the new dates cost more, you pay the difference. If they cost less, the difference is not refunded.

When you will get a refund

  • We cannot provide the room you booked, and you do not accept another room
  • You were charged twice for the same booking
  • A payment was taken but the booking was not created

In these cases we refund the full amount to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days of confirming the problem. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

How to ask

WhatsApp or call +91 87925 64056, or email hanumanheritage@gmail.com, with your booking ID and payment reference. We reply within 24 hours.
