About us

Last updated 19 November 2026

Hanuman Heritage is a hotel on Jyothinagar Main Road in Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka, next to Abharan Jewellers. We welcome couples, families, friends travelling together, business visitors and wedding guests.

What we offer

  • 26 rooms: 10 AC rooms, 12 non-AC rooms, 2 twin bedded AC rooms, and family rooms with four and seven beds
  • Family rooms for 4 and for 6 to 8 guests
  • WiFi and TV in every room, and a lift
  • An event hall for engagement ceremonies, birthdays, family functions and meetings

Booking with us

You can book on this website, by phone or on WhatsApp. Online bookings are paid securely through our payment partner Razorpay, and are confirmed straight away. See our room prices and terms.
